HealthCare AI Innovation Lab (HAIL)

Advancing healthcare through trustworthy artificial intelligence, data-driven research, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

About HAIL

HAIL is a research-focused innovation lab dedicated to developing impactful AI solutions for healthcare. We bring together students, researchers, clinicians, and industry partners to solve real-world medical challenges.

Focus Areas

  • Medical image analysis and diagnostics
  • Clinical decision support systems
  • Predictive analytics for patient outcomes
  • Responsible and explainable AI in healthcare
